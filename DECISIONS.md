## BOARDROOM MINUTES — 2026-08-24

### Round 1 — Scope war
**Visionary:** full couple platform + tracking + AI chat counsellor. **Skeptic (hit #1):** every feature that needs a partner's cooperation dies against the recon's core finding — the modal user is ALONE; and an AI chat counsellor is exactly the "AI slop" the founder banned; a chat can't guarantee evidence-grounding per sentence. **Market Analyst:** the winning wedge is the unserved solo stay/go/rishta question. **Engineer:** chat = unbounded LLM surface, impossible to anti-Barnum-verify; assessment→report is a bounded, verifiable pipeline. VERDICT: assessment→report pipeline is the product. Chat killed. Longitudinal tracking killed (v1).

**Couple mode fight:** Skeptic: "couple mode doubles surface area for a secondary user." Engineer: sealed URL-hash exchange costs ~1 screen + a diff engine, zero backend. Visionary + Target User: the founder's honesty standard (§4.1) is only fully answerable in couple mode — sealed blind exchange makes answer-copying structurally impossible (you commit before you can see theirs), and perception-GAP analysis (Joel 2020: perceptions are the signal) turns "cheating detection" into the product's most honest feature. VERDICT: couple mode ships in v1, deliberately lean: same assessment, sealed exchange, gap report.

### Round 2 — Length vs completion (Kano + pre-mortem)
**UX Director:** 45–60 min is the founder's floor AND our biggest death risk. Lane 4 gives the machinery: 5 named chapters, per-chapter progress, autosave/resume, chapter-boundary micro-insights, mirror reveal at 100%. **Skeptic (hit #2):** first-60-seconds law conflicts with a 50-min instrument — a new user must taste the wow BEFORE committing. Resolution (TRIZ, not compromise): a **2-minute "Jhalak" (glimpse)** — 8 questions → one honest micro-read with visible receipts + explicit "this is a glimpse; the full mirror needs the real thing" honest labelling. Depth preserved, wow moved to minute 1. VERDICT: Jhalak funnel + full 5-chapter assessment.

### Round 3 — Trust & delivery
**Data/Science seat:** every sentence traceable (instrument, citation, user's own answers) — renderer refuses uncited prose (anti-Barnum invariant, executable). **Story/Counsellor seat:** R1–R20 master sequence IS the report spec. **Skeptic (hit #3):** citations can read as academic coldness — kills the "homely" mandate. Resolution: receipts live behind a tap ("Yeh kaise pata?" expander) — warmth on the surface, science one tap below. **Resource Officer:** all free; LLM optional; template narrator is the guaranteed path.
