# Lane 4 — Long-Assessment Completion UX (verified 2026-08-24)

## Ranked design rules

1. **Chunk into named, meaningful sections** — never one 300-item wall. Multi-step beats single-page in ~70% of tests past ~7 fields. Gottman's 480-item Checkup survives via 5 named categories, multi-sitting. Name chapters after relationship domains, not "Part 3 of 9".
2. **Save-and-resume non-negotiable.** Galesic & Bosnjak 2009: dropout rises monotonically with length; quality degrades in later positions. Autosave every answer; state "answers saved automatically" upfront.
3. **Honest total time, framed in chapters.** "5 chapters, ~10 min each — finish one now, come back anytime."
4. **Per-chapter progress, not one global bar.** Villar et al. 2013 meta-analysis: slow honest global bar = highest abandonment (~22% vs 12.7% no bar). Use chapter dots + fast-filling within-chapter bar.
5. **One question per screen, tap-to-advance, zero typing.** Likert as tappable chips in thumb zone; open text optional. (Typeform/16personalities pattern; NN/g India.)
6. **Teaser micro-insights at chapter boundaries** — real, small insight after each chapter; break point = hook point. Stage the identity-reveal (16personalities' pull).
7. **Designed break points** between chapters ("chai break — sab saved hai"). Split-questionnaire research (GESIS): modularization cut drop-off ~12.5%; fallback if 60 min fatal: core module + rotated modules.
8. **Gamify the frame, not the answers.** Keusch & Zhang 2017: game elements lift fun/time-on-task; rewards tied to speed/content corrupt data (2024 ExSampling: rewards increased quantity, reduced reliability). Safe: chapter animations, narrative framing.
9. **2–3 validated attention checks max** (Instructed Response Items, never trick questions), one per ~20 min; exclude on ≥2 fails. Passive flags: sub-1s Likert response times, straight-lining within blocks (Zhang & Conrad).
10. **India mobile-first:** mid-range Android, resume via link (no login friction), Hinglish for encouragement microcopy ("Bas 2 chapters aur!", "Sab kuch save ho gaya hai"), questions in clean simple English.

## Recommended structure

- Screen 0: "5 chapters · ~10 min each · auto-saved · private" + one demo question before commitment.
- 5–6 named chapters, 8–12 min, 20–35 items each (How You Talk / How You Fight / Trust & Safety / Intimacy & Closeness / Money, Family & Future / You as an Individual). Sensitive chapters mid-sequence.
- Chapter boundary: completion animation → teaser micro-insight → Continue (primary) / Break (secondary). Resume deep-links to exact question.
- Quality layer: 3 IRIs; passive speeding + straight-lining flags; flag-don't-block, exclude on ≥2 failures.
- Finale: full "Aaina" mirror reveal only at 100%.
