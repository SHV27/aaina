# Lane 3 — India Market, Audience, Trust Mechanics (verified 2026-08-24)

## Competitors
- **AstroTalk**: FY25 revenue ₹1,214 cr (+85% YoY), 50M+ downloads, 8M+ users; relationship/marriage questions dominant. Kundli-matching owns the "compatibility report" mental model — Indians already pay for & share compatibility reports, just astrological ones.
- **Western couples apps** (all assume both partners + maintenance mode): Paired (recycled shallow questions), Lasting ($29.99/mo, homework fatigue), Relish (cloned "personal coach" trust failure), Agapé/Coral (novelty decay).
- **India mental-health players** sell sessions (YourDOST, Amaha, TalktoAngel ~₹797+): no free structured self-assessment. Wysa proves clinical-evidence framing works as Indian trust asset.
- **Free tests**: Gottman free quizzes (thin), Checkup (paid, therapist-gated), SEO quiz sites (generic, Western-normed, no receipts). 16personalities' shareable-type-card = gold-standard viral test UX, unapplied to relationships in India.
- **Structural gap = Aaina's lane:** solo user asking "should I stay/go / say yes to this rishta?" — the Indian modal question, served by nobody.

## Audience
- Arranged-marriage decision stress is the sharpest pain: "how do I decide after 2–3 meetings?", "should I cancel my wedding?", timeline panic, limited-information fear.
- Urban divorce up 30–50% over the decade; women initiate most urban proceedings; destigmatizing discourse.
- Therapy ₹1,500–5,000/session metros; couples ₹1,000–3,000/hr. ₹0 sits below every floor.
- Language: reads English, feels in Hinglish. English scaffold + Hinglish emotional resonance.

**Delighters (ranked):** verdict-with-reasons for stay/go/pause · arranged-marriage-literate questions · shareable beautiful result card · works solo · free without upsell ambush · specific personal language.
**Dealbreakers (ranked):** generic chatbot-grade advice · requiring partner join · fake-personal touches exposed · data-privacy fear on intimate answers · Western framing ignoring family · paywall after emotional investment.

## Trust mechanics (from AstroTalk, ethically reusable)
1. Anonymity is the product — no signup, data stays in browser, say so loudly.
2. Specificity feels true — earn it by reflecting user's own answers verbatim.
3. Visible vetting = authority — name the actual research with citations.
4. Ritual/ceremony — verdict lands as a moment, not a score dump.
5. First 60 seconds must deliver the "it sees me" jolt.
6. Users buy uncertainty relief — clear directional read + honest confidence level.
