# Lane 1 — Validated Instruments & Science (verified 2026-08-24)

## Recommended free-legal battery
CSI-16 (free) + ECR-S/ECR-RS (public domain) + CPQ-SF with Crenshaw 2017 revised scoring + Investment Model Scale short form + Joel et al. stay/leave reasons pool + WAST (widely reproduced, free with citation) for safety gating + 2–3 infrequency/instructed items + response-time & long-string checks + optional Marlowe-Crowne Form C.

## Instruments (license status inline)

- **CSI-16/32/4** — Funk & Rogge 2007, J Fam Psych 21(4). IRT-built, N=5,315. CSI-16: 16 items, 0–81, α=.98, distress cutoff <51.5. CSI-32 cutoff <104.5; CSI-4 cutoff <13.5. **Free for research/clinical use, no permission needed beyond citation** [VOLATILE 2026-08-24]. Field-standard satisfaction measure; superior to DAS/MAT.
- **ECR-R** (Fraley, Waller & Brennan 2000, JPSP 79) — 36 items, public domain (Fraley lab page). **ECR-S** (Wei et al. 2007, JPA 88) — 12 items (6 anxiety α=.77–.86, 6 avoidance α=.78–.88), free non-commercial with citation. **ECR-RS** (Fraley 2011, 9 items/domain, public domain). Treat anxiety/avoidance as continuous dimensions, never types.
- **CPQ / CPQ-SF** (Christensen 1987; Christensen & Heavey 1990) — demand/withdraw + mutual constructive communication, 9-pt. Use **Crenshaw et al. 2017 (Psych Assessment 29(7)) revised scoring** (fixes low reliability). License: unpublished academic instrument, freely circulated; historically free for research/clinical; confirm with Christensen lab for public app [VOLATILE]. No public-domain Four Horsemen questionnaire exists — Gottman self-report versions proprietary; operationalize via CPQ + bespoke items.
- **Investment Model Scale** (Rusbult, Martz & Agnew 1998, Personal Relationships 5) — satisfaction, alternatives, investment, commitment (7 items); α .82–.95; strong stay/leave predictive validity. Published in journal appendix, Agnew's lab distributes freely for research. Short form: Rodrigues & Lopes 2013.
- **Joel, MacDonald & Page-Gould 2018 (SPPS 9(6))** — open item pool: 27 stay reasons, 23 leave reasons. ~Half of leavers are above midpoint on BOTH (ambivalence is the norm); attachment anxiety → most stay+leave reasons. Joel et al. 2021 EJSP: ambivalent commitment swings daily. Published content, usable with citation. **Backbone of the clarity framing.**
- **IPV screening:** HITS is COPYRIGHTED (fee) — avoid. **WAST** (Brown et al. 1996, Fam Med 28:422) — 8 items, WAST-Short = first 2; sens ~47%, spec ~96%; widely reproduced, free with citation → use. CASR-SF (Ford-Gilboe 2016, BMJ Open) — no fee but permission required. Rabin et al. 2009: no IPV tool has fully established psychometrics — state this honestly. Positive screen ⇒ route to safety, never "work on it" advice.

## What predicts stay/leave (for scoring model + humility)
- **Le et al. 2010** meta-analysis (137 studies, N=37,761): strongest = commitment, love, IOS, dependence; moderate = satisfaction, alternatives, investment; robust external = social network support. Personality/attachment: small.
- **Joel et al. 2020 PNAS** (43 dyadic datasets, ML): top = perceived partner commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, perceived partner satisfaction, conflict. Own perceptions ≈ all signal; partner reports add little (**good news for solo mode**). ~45% variance baseline, ~18% longitudinal — **change over time largely unpredictable; verdict language must carry this humility.**
- **Karney & Bradbury 1995**: vulnerability–stress–adaptation model; no silver-bullet predictor.
- **Gottman criticisms:** 90%+ "divorce prediction" = postdiction without cross-validation; Heyman & Slep 2001 (JMF 63:473): dropped to ~29% PPV on holdout. Never claim Gottman-style accuracy. 5:1 ratio & contempt remain useful descriptively.

## Honesty machinery (what science actually allows)
- Marlowe-Crowne Form C (Reynolds 1982, J Clin Psych 38:119) — 13 items T/F, KR-20 ≈.76, r=.93 with full scale; free in practice. Caveat (Uziel 2010; McCrae & Costa 1983): partly measures real traits — use to FLAG/soften, never invalidate.
- Meade & Craig 2012 (Psych Methods 17:437): bogus/infrequency items, instructed response, long-string, even-odd consistency, Mahalanobis, response time (<~2s/item red flag); ~10–12% careless. Ward & Meade 2023 Annual Review update.
- **Detecting carelessness: well-supported. Detecting deliberate faking: weak, individual-level unreliable.** Honest design (privacy, "no right answers") reduces distortion more than detection. Product must state this.

## Avoid list (with citations)
- MBTI-style typing: Pittenger 1993/2005; Stein & Swan 2019 — 50% type-switch in 5 weeks.
- Five Love Languages: Impett, Park & Muise 2024 CDPS — no support for core claims; quiz proprietary.
- DAS/Locke-Wallace: superseded by CSI; DAS commercially licensed (MHS).
- Compatibility-percentage scores; exact divorce-probability claims.

## Clinician battery inspiration
Gottman Relationship Checkup (proprietary): ~337 items, 5 sections, IPV gating changes downstream recs, strengths listed alongside concerns. Research batteries (OurRelationship trials): CSI + CPQ + IMS + brief IPV — exactly our free stack.
