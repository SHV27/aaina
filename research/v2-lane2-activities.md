# v2 Lane 2 — Post-Assessment Activity Library (evidence-graded, verified 2026-08-24)

Ev grades: A = multiple RCTs/meta · B = ≥1 RCT or strong longitudinal · C = theory+correlational.

## Anchor programs
- **OurRelationship** (Doss 2016 JCCP; d=0.69; replicated low-income, coach-support variants): Observe → Understand (DEEP formulation: Differences, Emotional sensitivities, External stressors, Patterns) → Respond (share formulations; acceptance+change responses). THE template for the "work on it" track.
- **ePREP** (Braithwaite & Fincham 2007/2011 RCTs): danger signs psychoeducation (escalation, invalidation, withdrawal, negative interpretation), speaker–listener technique, time-out protocol, weekly homework. Reduced aggression at 10-month follow-up.
- **Marriage Checkup** (Cordova 2014 JCCP): assessment+strengths-first MI feedback IS an intervention (2-yr satisfaction gains) — deliver our report Checkup-style.

## By indication

### Low satisfaction (both leaning in)
- DEEP formulation + share — Ev A — contra: IPV, secret leaner-out — M·3×45m
- Finkel 21-minute reappraisal writing (neutral-third-party view of last conflict; 3×7m/yr eliminated decline; Psych Science 2013 N=120) — Ev B — solo-doable — L
- Active-Constructive Responding to good news (Gable 2004/2006 JPSP) — Ev B — contra: contempt-saturated — L·daily
- Expressed gratitude (Algoe find–remind–bind) — Ev B — L·5m×3/wk
- Caring Days behavioral exchange (Stuart 1980; BCT-supported) — Ev B — contra: scorekeeping, IPV — L·2wks

### Demand–withdraw / escalation
- Speaker–listener dialogue (PREP) — Ev A — contra: IPV — M·20m
- Soft startup ("I feel X about Y, I need Z"; harsh startup predicts divorce trajectory — Carrère & Gottman 1999 first-3-minutes) — Ev B — contra: IPV — L
- Repair attempts + flooding time-out (HR>100 → 20+min break with scheduled return) — Ev B/C — contra: stonewalling misuse — L
- IBCT unified detachment (pattern as "it"; Christensen 2004 RCT, better 5-yr maintenance) — Ev A — M·30m

### Attachment anxiety
- EFT Hold-Me-Tight conversation (EFT ~70-73% recovery, Johnson; HMT self-help trials Kennedy 2019) — Ev A/B — **contra: IPV + untreated addiction (Johnson's own exclusions)**, leaner-out partner — H·60m+
- IBCT empathic joining (soft emotion under hard) — Ev A — contra: IPV — M
- Security priming / felt-security writing (Mikulincer & Shaver) — Ev B — L·10m

### Avoidance / flatness / low intimacy
- Shared NOVEL-exciting activities (Aron 2000 JPSP; Coulter & Malouff 2013 4-wk RCT) — Ev A — contra: severe conflict — L·90m/wk
- 36 Questions (Aron 1997 PSPB — fully published) — Ev B — L
- Stress-reducing conversation (daily 15-20m about OUTSIDE stress; validate, no advice; Bodenmann CCET dyadic coping) — Ev B — contra: smuggling complaints — L·daily
- Partner-knowledge check-ins (love-maps CONCEPT via journal citations; no proprietary decks) — Ev C — L
- Sexual communication module (Mallory 2019/2022 metas, J Sex Research) — Ev B — contra: coercion history — M

### Ambivalence / leaning-out
- Discernment-derived three-paths structure w/ decision date + own-contribution focus (Doherty & Harris 2017; 2016 Family Process) — Ev C solo — **contra: IPV (explicitly contraindicated)** — M·2-4wks
- MI decisional-balance worksheet vs stated values (Ottawa decision-aid framework) — Ev C — honesty flag: NO published stay/leave decision-aid RCT exists — M·45m
- NEVER prescribe intimacy exercises (36Q/HMT) as leverage on an ambivalent partner (mixed-agenda misuse).

### Post-breakup
- Repeated structured self-reflection = measurement-as-intervention (Larson & Sbarra 2015 SPPS N=210: self-concept clarity ↑, loneliness ↓) — Ev B — L·15m weekly — the scientific basis for a "support system" check-in loop!
- NARRATIVE expressive writing (story arc), NOT open venting — Sbarra 2013: classic expressive writing HARMED high ruminators up to 9 months; Bourassa 2017 narrative form safe — Ev B — **gate on rumination** — M
- Self-concept rebuilding (Slotter 2010; Mason 2012) — Ev B — M·weeks
- Self-compassion practice (Sbarra 2012 Psych Science; Neff & Germer MSC RCT) — Ev B — L·daily
- Reduce cue exposure/ex-surveillance (Marshall 2012 correlational — frame honestly) — Ev C — contra: co-parents need structured contact
- Love-regulation via negative reappraisal (Langeslag & Sanchez 2018 JEP:G; mood dips short-term) — Ev B — L

### Danger (OVERRIDES EVERYTHING)
- myPlan-style safety decision aid (Glass 2017 RCT N=720: less decisional conflict, safer choices; Campbell Danger Assessment items) — Ev A
- **NOT prescribed: ALL conjoint exercises** (speaker-listener, HMT, DEEP-share, discernment) where coercive control/violence present (Johnson exclusions; Stith 2011); no venting writing; no "give it 6 months"; nothing requiring disclosure to the abuser.

## Cross-cutting rules
1. BCT sequencing: positivity-building BEFORE conflict-skills (distressed couples can't problem-solve first).
2. Solo-doable subset: Finkel reappraisal, ACR, gratitude, soft startup, self-compassion, decisional balance, all breakup items. HMT + speaker-listener need two willing partners.
3. IP hygiene: teach Gottman/PREP MECHANISMS from journal citations; never reproduce branded worksheets/card decks. Aron 36Q fully published.
4. Honesty flags in-product: no stay/leave decision-aid RCT; expressive writing conditionally harmful; "no contact" is correlational folk wisdom.
