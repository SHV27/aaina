## IMS global items (Rusbult, Martz & Agnew 1998) — 0–8 (Do Not Agree At All 0 · Agree Somewhat 4 · Agree Completely 8); subscale MEANS; commitment 2 reversed
Satisfaction (5): "I feel satisfied with our relationship." / "My relationship is much better than others' relationships." / "My relationship is close to ideal." / "Our relationship makes me very happy." / "Our relationship does a good job of fulfilling my needs for intimacy, companionship, etc."
Alternatives (5): "My alternatives are attractive to me (dating another, spending time with friends or on my own, etc.)." / "My alternatives to our relationship are close to ideal." / "If I weren't dating my partner, I would do fine—I would find another appealing person to date." / "The people other than my partner with whom I might become involved are very appealing." / "My needs for intimacy, companionship, etc., could easily be fulfilled in alternative relationships."
Investment (5): "I have put a great deal into our relationship that I would lose if the relationship were to end." / "Compared to other people I know, I have invested a great deal in my relationship with my partner." / "I feel very involved in our relationship—like I have put a great deal into it." / "Many aspects of my life have become linked to my partner (recreational activities, etc.), and I would lose all of this if we were to break up." / "My relationships with friends and family members would be complicated if my partner and I were to break up (e.g., partner is friends with people I care about)."
Commitment (7): "I am committed to maintaining my relationship with my partner." / "I want our relationship to last for a very long time." / "I feel very attached to our relationship—very strongly linked to my partner." / "It is likely that I will date someone other than my partner within the next year." **R** / "I would not feel very upset if our relationship were to end in the near future." **R** / "I want our relationship to last forever." / "I am oriented toward the long-term future of my relationship (for example, I imagine being with my partner several years from now)."

Note: v1 uses commitment (7) + satisfaction (5) + alternatives (5) + investment (5) = 22 global items. Facet items intentionally omitted (unscored in the published instrument).
Accuracy caveat: a few IMS phrase-tails were elided in mirrors; completions above are standard appendix wording (authoritative: doi:10.1111/j.1475-6811.1998.tb00177.x).
