# Lane 2 — Counsellor Delivery Craft → 20 implementable rules (verified 2026-08-24)

## A. Motivational Interviewing (Miller & Rollnick; SAMHSA TIP 35)
Direct confrontation triggers reactance (Brehm); MI beats confrontation in trials. OARS, rolling with resistance, Elicit-Provide-Elicit.
- **R1 AUTONOMY CLAUSE:** every verdict ends "only you can decide; here's what the evidence shows." No imperatives about the decision itself.
- **R2 ELICIT-PROVIDE-ELICIT:** reflect their own answers → present finding → reflective question. Their answers ARE the elicit.
- **R3 ROLL, DON'T ARGUE:** voice the likely objection sympathetically, then continue. Never rebut the reader.
- **R4 THEIR OWN WORDS:** quote user's inputs back as evidence — self-generated arguments beat author-generated.

## B. SPIKES (Baile & Buckman 2000, The Oncologist) adapted to written report
- **R5 WARNING SHOT** before hard verdicts ("what follows may be hard to read"). Never ambush.
- **R6 INVITATION GATE:** reader clicks/scrolls to reveal verdict — consent-to-know in UI.
- **R7 SMALL CHUNKS:** hard findings in short declarative sentences + whitespace pause before elaboration.
- **R8 EMPATHY BEFORE STRATEGY:** named-emotion acknowledgment right after verdict; every hard section ends with a next step.

## C. Feedback acceptance (Finn's Therapeutic Assessment; Swann self-verification; Sherman & Cohen self-affirmation)
- **R9 FINN'S LADDER:** order findings Level 1 (self-view-confirming) → 2 (mildly discrepant) → 3 (narrative-contradicting) last.
- **R10 AFFIRM FIRST, OFF-DOMAIN:** affirm a real strength OUTSIDE the relationship domain before hard news; same-domain affirmation backfires; must precede threat (Critcher timing).
- **R11 ANTI-BARNUM TEST:** every sentence must be false for some users; tie each claim to a specific answer given. If a horoscope could contain it, it doesn't ship.

## D. Pattern-not-person (EFT/Johnson; Gottman)
- **R12 PATTERN NOT PERSON:** name the cycle (e.g., pursue–withdraw) as the antagonist; character labels banned.
- **R13 BEHAVIOUR + INSTANCE + EFFECT:** "when X happens, you reported feeling Y" — never trait labels. EXCEPTION: abuse/coercion is never reframed as a mutual cycle — attributed to the behaviour of the person doing it.

## E. Discernment Counselling (Doherty, JMFT 2016) — "should I leave"
Three paths: status quo / separate / all-in time-boxed repair (~6 months) then decide. Leaning-in vs leaning-out. Clarity+confidence about DIRECTION, never a command.
- **R14 VERDICT ON EVIDENCE, NOT CHOICE:** confident about what the pattern is and where it leads; agnostic about what reader must do.
- **R15 THREE PATHS, EACH WITH A COST:** never binary stay/leave; status quo is also a choice with a price.
- **R16 TIME-BOXED TEST:** when repair plausible, bounded experiment (6 months, specific changes, reassess date).
- **R17 OWN-CONTRIBUTION MIRROR:** every report names the reader's contribution — inoculates against "app just validated me."

## F. Style for India-English
- **R18 GRADE-8 CEILING:** Flesch-Kincaid ≤8, sentences 15–20 words; hard findings in shortest sentences.
- **R19 WARM-FORMAL INDIAN REGISTER:** no US idiom; jargon only if defined in one line; arranged marriage / joint family / log-kya-kahenge named without judgment.
- **R20 SUPPORT, NOT SENSATION:** compassionate precision; negative-toned dramatization measurably worsens distress.

## Master report sequence
Affirm off-domain (R10) → Level-1 confirmations w/ quoted answers (R9,R4) → name the cycle (R12) → warning shot + invitation (R5–R6) → verdict in chunks + empathy (R7–R8) → three paths with costs (R15) → time-boxed test or exit resources (R16) → autonomy clause (R1).
