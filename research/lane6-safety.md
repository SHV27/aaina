## Design rules
1. **WHO LIVES** script at disclosure moment: Listen, Inquire, Validate, Enhance safety, Support. "It's not your fault", never "why don't you leave".
2. Resources trigger contextually on signal (fear, harm, coercive control, self-harm) + persistent low-key "Get help" entry.
3. **Quick-exit button** on IPV screens: one click/ESC → innocuous site, replace history entry; honest note it doesn't erase history/defeat monitoring (NNEDV).
4. **Never store danger disclosures**: process client-side, exclude safety section from saved/shared reports by default, no analytics on them.
5. **Privacy interstitial before sensitive sections**: "Are you somewhere private?" + skip option (partner may share device).
6. Every safety question skippable; screening without support pathway is unethical — resources appear immediately on positive screen.
7. Suicide signals → Tele-MANAS 14416 + AASRA/Vandrevala inline, phrased as care not alarm, zero extra taps.

## Legal paragraph (for reports)
PWDVA 2005: civil law; physical/sexual/verbal/emotional/economic abuse incl. live-in; protection orders, residence orders, monetary relief via Protection Officer/Magistrate/NGO without criminal case. 498A IPC (now BNS s.85/86): criminal cruelty, cognizable, non-bailable; Arnesh Kumar guidelines curbed automatic arrest. Mention both neutrally; app never gives legal advice.
