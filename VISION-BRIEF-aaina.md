# VISION BRIEF — AAINA

**Working name:** Aaina (Hindi: *mirror*). Chosen for the founder's core promise — it shows you
what is actually there, without flattery and without pakhand, and a mirror works even when only
one person is standing in front of it. **The name is a working name only.** The team must verify
availability (domain, app stores, trademark, existing Indian apps) during recon and propose a
replacement if it is taken. Do not spend build hours on branding before the product is real.

**Founder:** Shaurya Verma (Showie). Final-year B.Tech CSE, TIET Patiala. Directs AI teams; does
not hand-write code. **He cannot audit psychology, psychometrics, or clinical correctness
himself.** See "Verification burden" below — this changes what you owe him.

**Mode:** FIRST BRIEF. Nothing exists yet. Start from zero.

## 1. The vision, in his words

> "vision hai ek software banana jo ek online relationship councellor bann jaye, compatibility
> analysis kar sake in depth, report de sake bahut detailed and intuitive, jo sabhi doubts clear
> karde ek couple ki"

> "knowledge kills ambiguity and uncertainity, and if knowledge nhi karta ye kaam toh bass we
> gotta use something that kills these two things at any cost"

> "this app is for everyone, great couples, bad couples, okay okay couples, couples who lost

> letting go is better, and for the toxic ones who should leave each other"

> "logon ka stress dur hona chahiye, ambiguity stress should be killed because I have seen
> people getting destroyed and I was one of them, wasted my whole year just to realise letting
> go was the option I should have chosen earlier"

> "this should be a councellor that listens and helps and gives clarity not any kind of ai slop"

The enemy, named explicitly: **AstroTalk and the pseudoscience economy.**

> "I have seen my friends wasting thousands of rupees on platforms like astrotalk joki everyone
> knows reality ki are fake... nobody is using the scientific way .. and this app is for this,
> pakandh rokne ke liye"

**Origin (context for you, NOT for the product):** the felt reference is the career-counselling
platform the founder's father uses professionally — a 60–90 minute test that produces an
extremely detailed report (aptitude, interests, personality, visual data interpretation) which
resolves a student's anxiety about which stream to take. That *report experience* is the bar.
**Hard constraint: the father is not mentioned anywhere in the product** — no attribution,
no persona, no branding, no "built with a counsellor" line. He is not part of the product.

## 3. Who it is for

- **Design center (his answer, verbatim): "a and c — mainly those jinhone decision lene hain
  abhi, ye main hain."** Unmarried/dating people stuck in doubt, and people deciding whether to
  commit (including pre-marriage / arranged-match decisions). These two win when priorities
  collide.
- **But it must not exclude anyone:** "vaise toh app kar kuch cater karre, like kisi bhi stage
  mein ho this app should help." Married, long-term, lost-the-spark, toxic — all in scope.
- **Solo users are first-class, not a fallback.** "zyada tar couples sath mein nhi denge, kyi
  baar sirf ek ladka ya ek ladki hi apne partner ko leke unsure hote hain."
- **Market:** India. Every age.
- **Positioning:** free public good. Reach and trust over revenue. No revenue model in v1.

## 4. Non-negotiable standards (his words, unedited)

1. **Honesty enforcement.** "make sure karenge ki couples somehow honest rhein ek dusre ke sath,
   like cheating na ho kisi tareeke ki, ki ek dusre ka answer dekh ke cheat karne ka ho" — and
   the harder one: "basic basic cheezein couples ko ek dusre ke baare mein bahut ache se pata
   hoti hain, those can be faked to get a high score, so saare tarah ke swaal hon but something
   aisa zarur ho jisse fake na kiya jaa sake." **See §6 — this standard is partly unreachable.**
2. **Depth.** "super detailed, super duper detailed easy to understand intuitive." The report
   must clear every doubt a couple has, with real data interpretation, visually — the way the
   career report he described does.
3. **Counsellor craft over generic advice.** "voh generic baatein nhi karte I guess, you gotta
   study how they approach every kind of situation because people are repulsive or resistant to
   generic baatein... councellors ka apna way hota hai har baat convey karne ka insaan ko problem
   se nikaalne ka." **You are required to study how real counsellors deliver hard truths** and
   build that craft into the output. Generic advice = failure.
4. **Calm delivery.** "ye councellor report ke through ya kaise bhi bass calmness provide kar
   sake, homly tareeke se samjha sake, ki reader panick na kare." Lovable, homely, professional.
5. **Actionability in every direction.** "if behtar bann sakta hai rishta toh kaise, if theek
   karna hai toh kaise, if nhi karna chahiye theek toh kya, sambhalna kaise ye voh etc, sab ho
   usmein." Including how to survive an ending.
6. **Anti-slop.** No filler, no horoscope-shaped vagueness, no LLM padding.

## 6. Standards that reality will not fully grant (route honestly, do not fake)

The founder was told this in chat and it is recorded here so a stranger reading this brief does
not chase the impossible:

- **"Aisa question jo fake na kiya ja sake" cannot be fully achieved by self-report.** A
  motivated liar can defeat any questionnaire. The literature offers detection, not prevention,
  and detection is probabilistic. **Get as close as the science genuinely allows. Then state
  plainly, inside the product, what the honesty machinery can and cannot see.** Never imply
  lie-proofness. Overclaiming here turns us into AstroTalk with better fonts — which is the one
  outcome the founder would consider total failure.
- **Solo results cannot be as reliable as two-sided results.** They can be genuinely useful and
  genuinely decisive. They cannot be equivalent, because one person's perception is the only
  input. Deliver the confident verdict he asked for; be honest about the input.
- **Where you hit either wall, produce evidence he can show to a skeptic** — not a reassurance.

## 7. Verification burden (read this twice)

He said it himself: **"I dont know how would you achieve this vision."** He cannot check your
psychology, your instrument design, your scoring, or your interpretation logic. Therefore:

- **Every psychological claim, construct, scale, threshold, and interpretation rule the product
  makes must be traceable to a named, published, peer-reviewed source, listed in a document he
  can hand to a professional.** If you cannot source it, the product does not say it.
- **Be actively skeptical of popular-but-weak instruments.** Type-based personality systems that
  are commercially popular in India are not automatically valid; decision 3(a) binds you to the
  evidence, not to what looks impressive in a report.
- Verify with live tools and real runs, not just passing tests. He wants to see it work, not
  read that it works.
- Produce the evidence in a form he can hold up to someone else — because one day someone will
  ask him "iska base kya hai" and he must have an answer that isn't "the AI said so."

## 8. Autonomy charter

Decide, log, and continue on everything. **Escalate to him only for:**

1. Anything that would cost money.
2. Anything that changes who the product is for or what it promises.
3. Discovering that decision 3's two halves (defensibility + warmth) genuinely cannot coexist
   somewhere important.
4. The safety conflict in decision 7.
5. A finding that the anti-faking machinery is weaker than the product's promise implies.

Everything else — research, debate, priorities, stack, architecture, data model, art direction,
report structure, question design, arc plan — **is yours to decide, and you are expected to
decide it better than this brief could.** Log your reasoning. Keep PROGRESS.md current so any
session can resume cold.
